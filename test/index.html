Log colours

What each colour in the message log means.
BlueSomething the page is about to do (reading, connecting).
GreenFinished and confirmed by the pedal.
AmberWorth noticing: refused action, pedal unplugged, change made on the pedal.
RedError: the message failed or the pedal did not accept it.
PurpleRaw MIDI bytes sent and received.
GreyTime the line was logged.
